Assessment

The Clock That Stopped · independent full short story · 40 marks. Score the finished pupil story, not its planning notes.

Criterion marking

Performance bands

Apply these descriptions proportionately to each criterion; award integer marks. They guide professional judgement and are not an automatic percentage formula.

  • Consistently Accomplished: Sustained, purposeful and controlled execution; effective choices with few meaningful lapses.
  • Secure: Mostly convincing control with clear strengths; some uneven development or accuracy.
  • Developing: Some effective moments but inconsistencies in organisation, detail or technical control.
  • Emerging: Fragmented or underdeveloped but some demonstrable relevant skill.
  • Limited: Very little evidence of the criterion; award 0 when there is no relevant attempt.

Marking protocol

  1. Read the whole response before scoring. Assess content/organisation out of 24 marks and technical accuracy out of 16 marks separately.
  2. For each criterion, award an integer from 0 to the stated maximum, using band descriptions as broad guidance rather than an automatic formula.
  3. Record at least two specific strengths and two precise next-step improvements, supported by quoted or pinpointed passages in the pupil writing.
  4. Multiple story interpretations are valid. Do not penalise an unresolved mystery if it is an intentional, coherent ending, but do distinguish this from an unfinished story.
  5. Do not penalise non-use of dialogue, strict 350-word minimum, or a title; neither is compulsory.
  6. The standalone paper exports an attempt. A 70% threshold would be 28/40 but no automated result, website hand-in or pass status may be claimed until authorised grading and the website policy are integrated.
