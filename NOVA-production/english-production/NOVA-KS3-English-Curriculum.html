NOVA English

Key Stage 3 Curriculum -- Years 7, 8 and 9

What this curriculum covers

Four strands taught every year -- Reading, Writing, Grammar & Vocabulary, and Spoken English -- built around whole texts, poetry, non-fiction and two full Shakespeare plays across the three years. Year 8 reuses and extends material already built for [[son]]'s existing course; Year 7 and Year 9 are built fresh around it.

Independent reading -- 3x a week, outside the numbered lessons. Free choice of book, logged not tested.
Weekly spelling -- new key words drawn from that week's lesson topic or class text, tested weekly. Supports SPaG (spelling, punctuation and grammar) alongside the Grammar & Vocabulary strand woven through every block.
