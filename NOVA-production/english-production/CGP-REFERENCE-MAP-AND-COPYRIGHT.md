## Source precedence and lawful transformation

The canonical `NOVA-KS3-English-Curriculum.html` and `NOVA-KS3-English-Lesson-Plan-1.html` control what/when. CGP controls neither lesson numbering nor Challenge assignment. Copyright-safe use is to abstract the teaching principle (e.g. model how scanning works, then ask a new evidence-finding question using a completely new passage), not paraphrase a distinctive CGP passage or redraw its picture. Use properly licensed or public-domain literary extracts, or original texts, with attribution and rights information as appropriate.

For each lesson, Run 1 notes *actual relevant CGP topic areas* and whether pages were accessible; Run 2 records **NOVA-original content checks**. No CGP PDFs should be committed to the **public** `notdamien2009/maths-v2` repository, and don't place copyrighted screenshots or answers there.
