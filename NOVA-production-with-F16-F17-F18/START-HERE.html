What to try

Start → Learn with Bob → Continue to Learn. For each model, try Watch, change its numbers in Explore, and complete its six-question Challenge. Try a wrong answer, a hint and New game. Then open Examples, Practice and Exit.

The full lesson has 5 Start, 15 Practice and 10 Exit questions. Numeric and choice responses are marked automatically; written explanations are saved for teacher/parent review.
