Developer: component specification

Purpose: reflect polygons in vertical, horizontal and diagonal mirror lines; preserve congruence and show equal perpendicular distances. Years: 7–9, GCSE extension. Presets: y-axis reflection, x = 1, x-axis reflection, y = x. Interaction: snapped mouse, touch and keyboard translation of image and vertical/horizontal mirror handles; mouse capture cancellation and Escape restore state. Correct: exact image position with all corresponding vertices in the correct locations; coordinate questions checked mathematically. Misconceptions: moving instead of flipping, changing only one coordinate, using incorrect distance from a non-zero mirror line, reversing vertex order. Linked: F0-02, F0-04, F10-01, F10-02, F14-02, F14-03.
