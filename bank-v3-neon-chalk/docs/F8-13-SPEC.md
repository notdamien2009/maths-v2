# NOVA F8-13 — Algebraic Index Laws (F3-10 preset)

**Source contract:** `NOVA-MASTER-FOR-OTHER-AI.md`, F8-13, 11 October 2026; F0 Neon Tube + Chalk and `F3-10 Index Law Animator` are the canonical dependencies. **This is a preset, not a separate production engine.**

## Purpose
Y8-002 Laws of indices. Show how identical algebraic bases are retained and exponents count factors:

- multiplication: a³ × a² = a⁵ (add indices), y² × y⁴ = y⁶;
- division: b⁵ ÷ b² = b³ and p⁵ ÷ p³ = p² (subtract indices; base must be non-zero);
- power of a power: (x²)³ = x⁶ and (m³)² = m⁶ (multiply indices).

## Misconceptions
Wrong results are diagnosed: adding when a power is raised to a power, multiplying when same-base powers multiply, adding when dividing, or dividing the indices. Non-zero restriction is stated for cancellation.

## GCSE extension
This component establishes positive integer powers; zero/negative/fractional indices need separately checked representations. The original F3-10 retains its Year 9 fractional index preset unchanged.
