## Provisional alignment decision
The assessment title makes a **written extended response to a new short story** the primary measure. Transfer from inference, precise quotation, language effect and analytical paragraph writing is central. Sentence control, word choice and editing may be assessed within writing or through a short accuracy item. The earlier presentation/discussion lessons remain genuine unit learning but should not be falsely graded through an unrelated written question. Narrative description can inform appreciation of writer choices, but assessment should not become a brand-new creative-writing exam.
