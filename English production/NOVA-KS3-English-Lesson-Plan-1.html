NOVA English

Key Stage 3 Lesson Plan -- Years 7 to 9, self-paced

How this plan works

Self-paced, not a weekly timetable. Lessons are numbered in teaching order only -- no lesson is tied to a calendar week. Complete lessons in order within a year; move to the next year as soon as the current one is finished.

Independent reading -- 3x a week, outside the numbered lessons. Free choice of book, logged not tested.
Weekly spelling -- new key words drawn from that week's lesson topic or class text, tested weekly. Supports SPaG (spelling, punctuation and grammar) alongside the Grammar & Vocabulary strand woven through every block.
